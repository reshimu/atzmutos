7 · Rules That Don't Bend

  • Record long, cut later. Storage is cheap; a missed whale join is gone forever.
  • Move slowly. Fast mouse movement is unusable. Every motion at half your normal speed.
  • Pause after every click — 4 seconds. Still frames are what let the editor zoom, hold, and caption.
  • Never narrate or apologize on the mic. No VO in v1; audio gets discarded.
  • Don't chase perfection live. A missed feature can be re-shot with a scripted Playwright pass later. Live moments cannot.
